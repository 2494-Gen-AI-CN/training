## Weekly Epic

By Friday a learner can do two things in a Colab notebook. First, on a table with categories, blanks and extreme values, they can leave out the columns a lender may not decide on, engineer and encode features inside a scikit-learn `Pipeline` fitted on the training rows only, choose between dropping, filling and flagging blanks and between keeping, clipping and logging outliers, rank features and measure whether dropping some helps, pick the metric and the decision threshold that the cost of each mistake calls for, regularise a scikit-learn model and a Keras network, and report a cross-validated score as a mean and a spread before using the test set once. Monday's notebook does all of this on a 1,000-row credit table, and the learner applies it to their own Project 1 notebook in the afternoon clinic. Second, they can turn short texts into vectors with a sentence-embedding model and keep them in two vector databases: Chroma on Colab's disk and an index in their own Pinecone project, with the key read from Colab Secrets. In both they can create, read, update, upsert and delete records, manage collections and indexes, partition records by collection, namespace or metadata filter, and pick a distance metric. By Thursday they have built a semantic search over 1,000 forum posts: cleaned, tagged with named entities as metadata, embedded and upserted in batches, then queried with filters. The artifacts are the learner's Colab copies of the four notebooks and their finished Project 1 notebook, deck and README, presented on Friday. Project 1 (solo, showcase W8D5) needs Monday's metric, threshold and regularisation work for its evaluation and improvement attempt. Week 9's data connections (w09d2: text embedding, vector store, retrievers) start from the vector stores and the `search()` function that Tuesday to Thursday build.
