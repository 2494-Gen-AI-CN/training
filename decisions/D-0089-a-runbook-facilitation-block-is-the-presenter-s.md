# D-0089 — A runbook Facilitation block is the presenter's lecture notes, not an activity plan: topics introduced in order, one Topic each with Teach, Board, Ask and Bridge, at most one Activity; a notebook section opens with a Teach line

- Date: 2026-11-18
- Status: accepted
- Decision: A runbook Facilitation block is the presenter's lecture notes, not an activity plan: topics introduced in order, one Topic each with Teach, Board, Ask and Bridge, at most one Activity; a notebook section opens with a Teach line
- Why: Trainer feedback on 2026-11-18: the facilitations read as 'draw stuff on the board' and 'ask this question' and never say which topics are introduced or what the trainer is trying to say, so they are too vague to deliver from. The teaching content sat in the brief, the notes page and the hand-made week-6 crib sheets, not in the file the trainer presents from.
- Affects: harness/critics/runbook.md (items 3b, 3c, 6), harness/templates/runbook.md, harness/workflows/day-fanout.js (runbook author and humanizer prompts), AGENTS.md, trainer/week-06 runbooks for W6D4 and W6D5 (Facilitation blocks rewritten by hand), every runbook authored from week 7
- Supersedes: —
- Superseded-by: —
