# D-0060 — Notes prose carries a readability bar (metrics.notes.prose): paragraphs of at most 80 prose words, sentences of at most 40 words and 5 code spans outside the interview callouts and check answers, at least 3 lists or tables per page, a Takeaways recap (div.recap, 2-4 one-line items) before every interview callout, every code block followed by its output (pre.out or one sentence), and glossary tooltips underlined once per step; checked by notes_check.py --style (binding through notes-verify), guarded on live pages by prose_guard.py --restructure

- Date: 2026-10-17
- Status: accepted
- Decision: Notes prose carries a readability bar (metrics.notes.prose): paragraphs of at most 80 prose words, sentences of at most 40 words and 5 code spans outside the interview callouts and check answers, at least 3 lists or tables per page, a Takeaways recap (div.recap, 2-4 one-line items) before every interview callout, every code block followed by its output (pre.out or one sentence), and glossary tooltips underlined once per step; checked by notes_check.py --style (binding through notes-verify), guarded on live pages by prose_guard.py --restructure
- Why: Measured on the 9 live pages 2026-10-17: 153 of 575 teaching paragraphs run over 80 prose words (longest 156), 32 sentences run over 40 words (nearly all enumerations written as prose), 24 sentences carry more than 5 inline code spans, 6 pages have no list or table inside a teaching step, no step says what it taught, and glossary.js underlines every occurrence (325 underlines on W2D1, 'module' 76 times) so the tooltip loses its signal. Sentence length itself is fine (median 18 words), so the fix is paragraph shape, texture and a skim layer, not a controlled-language standard; an ASD-STE100 subset was weighed and set aside (dictionary is aerospace, procedure-shaped prose conflicts with the depth bar and critic item 18). The caps are hard from the week-3 batch; the live pages are restructured by a prose-guarded pass that may split paragraphs, listify enumerations and add recaps but cannot touch code, headings, citations, numbers or facts.
