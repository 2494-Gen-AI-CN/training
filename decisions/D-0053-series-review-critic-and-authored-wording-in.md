- Decision: Series review critic and authored wording in the week-plan critic: config.json models.stages gains series-review (opus, medium); when a day-fanout run covers more than one day, one critic agent runs at the end of the Demos phase, reads the week plan and every demo script of the run in ladder order against harness/critics/series-review.md and checks that the series tells one story, each rung starts where the previous one ended, the anti-spoiler ledger is respected and the last rung delivers the Weekly Epic; its findings are flags in the run record and the final report only, with no fix loop and nothing blocked; the week-plan critic, the week-plan author prompt and template and the demos critic read an authored day like a live one (recorded as-is)
- Why: A batch authors a week's rungs one day at a time and each day's critic sees one day; nobody checks that the rungs of the week add up to the Weekly Epic until the trainer reads them. A single critic over the whole series catches a broken story before the week is released, and flags only keep the run's cost bounded until the flag counts say a fix loop is worth it. With the authored status of D-0052 a hidden week's days must be treated as recorded-as-is by the plan critics, as live days are.
