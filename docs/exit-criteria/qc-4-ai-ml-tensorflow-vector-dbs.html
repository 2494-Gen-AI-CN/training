Week 6 · Day 1 — ML introduction & supervised learning

  • qc4.ai-ml-fundamentals.R01 Required Can explain what machine learning is, how a rule learned from examples differs from a rule written by hand, and define sample, feature, label and model.
  • qc4.ai-ml-fundamentals.R02 Required Can differentiate between the supervised and unsupervised learning paradigms, and between regression and classification, and give an example problem for each.
  • qc4.ai-ml-fundamentals.R03 Required Can build a Linear Regression model to predict a continuous target variable, explain how the line is fitted (slope, intercept, residuals, least squares) and interpret mean squared error and R².
  • qc4.ai-ml-fundamentals.R04 Required Can implement a train, validation and test split and explain the role of each part, including why a model is scored on data it was not trained on and why the test set is used once.
  • qc4.ai-ml-fundamentals.R05 Required Can describe scikit-learn's fit and predict pattern and use train_test_split, including what random_state and stratify do.
  • qc4.ai-ml-fundamentals.R06 Required Can identify signs of the bias-variance tradeoff, underfitting and overfitting from training and validation scores.
  • qc4.ai-ml-fundamentals.R07 Required Can build a Logistic Regression model for a binary classification task and explain the probability it outputs, how predict turns that probability into a class at 0.5, and the decision boundary.
  • qc4.ai-ml-fundamentals.R08 Required Can distinguish a model's parameters from its hyperparameters and give an example of each.
  • qc4.ai-ml-fundamentals.P01 Preferred Can explain multiple and polynomial regression and read a fitted model's coefficients.
  • qc4.ai-ml-fundamentals.P03 Preferred Can give examples of regression and classification problems in industry and say what a wrong answer costs in each.
  • qc4.ai-ml-fundamentals.S01 Stretch Can explain why a straight-line model is unreliable outside the range of its training data.

Week 6 · Day 2 — Unsupervised learning: clustering

  • qc4.ai-ml-fundamentals.R09 Required Can explain what clustering is, name uses of it, and explain how a clustering is judged when there are no labels.
  • qc4.ai-ml-fundamentals.R10 Required Can describe the k-means algorithm step by step (assign, update, repeat) and explain why the starting centroids matter.
  • qc4.ai-ml-fundamentals.R11 Required Can choose a value of K using the elbow method and the silhouette score, and explain what inertia measures.
  • qc4.ai-ml-fundamentals.R12 Required Can explain why features are scaled before a distance-based algorithm and what StandardScaler does.
  • qc4.ai-ml-fundamentals.P02 Preferred Can explain how a decision tree makes a prediction and how its depth relates to overfitting.
  • qc4.ai-ml-fundamentals.P04 Preferred Can compute Euclidean and Manhattan distance between two points.
  • qc4.ai-ml-fundamentals.P05 Preferred Can explain hierarchical clustering, name common linkage methods, and describe how a dendrogram is read and cut.
  • qc4.ai-ml-fundamentals.P06 Preferred Can describe where k-means fails and how DBSCAN differs (dense regions, noise points, eps and min_samples).
  • qc4.ai-ml-fundamentals.S02 Stretch Can run two iterations of k-means by hand on a small set of points.

Week 6 · Day 3 — Introduction to deep learning

  • qc4.ai-ml-fundamentals.R13 Required Can explain what deep learning is, how learned features differ from hand-made features, and when deep learning is and is not a good fit.
  • qc4.ai-ml-fundamentals.R14 Required Can name the main neural network families (feedforward, convolutional, recurrent, transformer, autoencoder) and match each to the kind of data it suits.
  • qc4.ai-ml-fundamentals.R15 Required Can describe a neuron (weighted sum, bias, activation), a layer and a forward pass, and count the parameters of a Dense layer.
  • qc4.ai-ml-fundamentals.R16 Required Can explain how a CNN reads an image (kernels, convolution, ReLU, max pooling) and why it suits images better than a flat Dense network.
  • qc4.ai-ml-fundamentals.R17 Required Can explain how text becomes numbers: tokens, a vocabulary, bag of words and TF-IDF.
  • qc4.ai-ml-fundamentals.P07 Preferred Can compute the output size of a convolution and of a pooling layer.
  • qc4.ai-ml-fundamentals.P08 Preferred Can explain n-grams and what bag of words loses.
  • qc4.ai-ml-fundamentals.P09 Preferred Can describe the steps of a text classification pipeline and why new text goes through transform, not fit.
  • qc4.ai-ml-fundamentals.P10 Preferred Can explain at a high level why self-attention handles distant positions better than recurrence, and the role of positional encoding in the Transformer architecture.
  • qc4.ai-ml-fundamentals.S03 Stretch Can explain why the operation deep learning libraries call convolution is cross-correlation.

Week 6 · Day 4 — TensorFlow introduction

  • qc4.tensorflow-and-keras.R01 Required Can explain what TensorFlow and Keras are and how they relate.
  • qc4.tensorflow-and-keras.R02 Required Can describe a tensor by its rank, shape and dtype and explain elementwise arithmetic, broadcasting and matrix multiplication.
  • qc4.tensorflow-and-keras.P01 Preferred Can explain eager execution, what tf.function does, and what tracing and retracing mean.
  • qc4.tensorflow-and-keras.P02 Preferred Can reduce a tensor along an axis (reduce_sum, reduce_mean, argmax) and explain keepdims.

Week 6 · Day 5 — Perceptrons & TensorFlow basics

  • qc4.ai-ml-fundamentals.R18 Required Can explain what an activation function does, describe sigmoid, tanh, ReLU and softmax, and explain why the activation must be non-linear.
  • qc4.ai-ml-fundamentals.R19 Required Can describe the mathematical formulation of a Perceptron (weighted sum, bias, step) and its learning rule, and explain linear separability and what a single perceptron cannot learn.
  • qc4.tensorflow-and-keras.R03 Required Can explain the difference between tf.constant and tf.Variable and what trainable means.

  • qc4.tensorflow-and-keras.R05 Required Can read model.summary(): the layers, their output shapes and their parameter counts.
  • qc4.tensorflow-and-keras.S01 Stretch Can explain how TensorFlow 1 sessions and placeholders differ from TensorFlow 2, enough to read old code.
